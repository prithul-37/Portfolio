The dev behind
the games

I'm Prithul Biswas Dip — a Unity game developer who's just as happy shipping a live title as I am getting lost in a boss fight, a fresh anime, or a late-night movie. That pull toward great stories and playful experiences is exactly what got me making games in the first place.

I build and release mobile games end to end. I'm core developer on the live hybrid-casual titles Pop'n Sort and Supermarket Sort Factory prototyped in collaboration with Supersonic and Moonee, and published under Riseup Labs on Google Play and the App Store. I own the whole client side of a title end to end: architecting gameplay systems and UI, building data-driven content, wiring up SDK integrations, and shipping releases to the stores and I use Claude Code daily in production, including custom AI-assisted level-design pipelines that speed up content.

Based in Dhaka, Bangladesh
